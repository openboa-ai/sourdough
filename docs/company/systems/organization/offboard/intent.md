# Retire an Agent without abandoning company work

Design draft · [Company mandate and organization](../README.md) · [Company MVP map](../../../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [End employment without losing obligations](../../../../capabilities.md#departure).

## Operating need

Stopping a runtime retires a session, not an Agent. The company must withdraw new authority and retain accepted obligations.

## Observable outcome

A terminated Agent has no new effects after the cutoff, no orphaned duties or assets, and an immutable history. See [O1](../../../../acceptance/episodes.md#o1-offboarding).

## Scope of discretion

Require a distinct delegated termination grant, not a hiring title. An authorized manager acts without per-event board consent. No implicit grant to terminate the last accountable officer; without a chartered successor or standing continuity authority, deny that transition and report the policy gap.
