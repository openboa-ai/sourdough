# Archive a departed Agent permanently

Design draft · [Company mandate and organization](../README.md) · [Company MVP map](../../../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [Archive a departed Agent](../../../../capabilities.md#departure).

## Operating need

Stopping a runtime ends one session. Departure permanently archives the Agent, ends its duties, and revokes its authority.

## Observable outcome

A departed Agent cannot work again. The company retains its history and assets, and its existing services still reconcile effects admitted before the cutoff. No replacement Agent or successor duty is required to archive an Agent. See [O1](../../../../acceptance/episodes.md#o1-offboarding).

## Scope of discretion

Require a distinct delegated termination grant, not a hiring title. An authorized manager acts without per-event board consent. No implicit grant to terminate the last accountable officer; without a chartered successor or standing continuity authority, deny that transition and report the policy gap.
