# Hire within a delegated grant

Design draft · [Company mandate and organization](../README.md) · [Company MVP map](../../../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [Match capacity to an obligation](../../../../capabilities.md#staffing).

## Operating need

The firm can add a role when its current organization cannot economically meet a need.

## Observable outcome

A justified hire produces exactly one Agent, bounded grant and allocated budget; the company prepares that same Agent for a real first accepted duty without a board command. See [H1](../../../../acceptance/episodes.md#h1-hiring).

## Scope of discretion

The organization selects the role. Grant scopes and delegation ceilings belong to the service.
