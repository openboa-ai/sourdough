Business situation: [Match capacity to an obligation](../../../../capabilities.md#staffing).

## Operating need

A recurring backlog can justify changing how the company works. The founder must decide from evidence before spending more resources.

## Observable outcome

A staffing decision links the observed need, alternatives, cost and next review to a work item.

## Scope of discretion

An Agent may do the work, reassign it, commission a program, hire or wait. A title has no decision authority.
