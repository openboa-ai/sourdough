Business situation: [Set direction and inspect it](../../../../capabilities.md#mandate).

## Operating need

The board delegates an objective and bounded authority. Business profiles bind their own resources, such as simulated capital. The same company can reopen while the board is away.

## Observable outcome

Opening the local service yields one charter, founder, grant and due work item without a market or venue. Profile bindings are separate from founding.

## Scope of discretion

The board owns purpose and grant ceilings. The founder chooses which useful work follows.
