# Give Agents durable company-owned workspaces

Design draft · [Company resources and personal workspaces](../README.md) · [Company MVP map](../../../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [Work alone or with others](../../../../capabilities.md#collaboration).

## Operating need

A departing Agent or lost runtime must not take the firm's research, code, credentials or open obligations with it.

## Observable outcome

The assigned workspace and repository remain discoverable and a successor can resume the active revision with bounded access.

## Scope of discretion

The company can assign isolated local workspaces, shared project repositories or dedicated machines behind the same ownership contract.

An Agent may develop or propose company tools, strategy code or connector adapters. Ownership and operating responsibility follow the asset and its business domain, not the author's role. Sharing a candidate does not activate it; see [company change activation](../../operations/manage-change/intent.md) and [resource provisioning](../provision-resource/intent.md).
