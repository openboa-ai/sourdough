# Allocate and consume operating budget

Design draft · [Finance and treasury](../README.md) · [Company MVP map](../../../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [Steward resources and explain outcomes](../../../../capabilities.md#stewardship).

## Operating need

Cash in the bank does not authorize another Agent, model turn or workspace.

## Observable outcome

A bounded envelope limits commitments across Agents and programs and explains remaining budget.

## Scope of discretion

An allocation is permission to incur cost, not liquid cash, a posted expense or trading headroom.
