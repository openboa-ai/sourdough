# Explain liquidity and near-term obligations

Design draft · [Finance and treasury](../README.md) · [Company MVP map](../../../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [Steward resources and explain outcomes](../../../../capabilities.md#stewardship).

## Operating need

Even a profitable company can lack available cash when an obligation falls due.

## Observable outcome

Finance reports available cash, upcoming obligations, uncertainty and projected shortfall over a declared horizon.

## Scope of discretion

Forecasts are not accounting entries or transfers. External banking and payroll are not implied.
