# Hand work to a colleague

Design draft · [Work and Agent communication](../README.md) · [Company MVP map](../../../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [Work alone or with others](../../../../capabilities.md#collaboration).

## Operating need

A colleague must be able to use evidence, ask for clarification and take responsibility while its author is offline.

A request for a colleague's decision can block existing work without transferring it. The requester must be able to leave that work waiting and resume it when the decision or a recorded refusal becomes available.

## Observable outcome

Work shows requested, accepted, delivered and verified outcomes with one accountable owner at each handoff.

## Scope of discretion

Reading a message does not imply acceptance or completion. Agents choose when collaboration helps.

Sharing a permitted artifact does not itself transfer a duty or expose the author's desk. Work owns acceptance and the current accountable owner; Resources and Agent Desk own custody and publication.
