# Choose the next useful job

Design draft · [Work and Agent communication](../README.md) · [Company MVP map](../../../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [Choose and perform work](../../../../capabilities.md#judgment).

## Operating need

Given the charter, obligations, available observations and cost, the Agent identifies work without a board message. A work decision retains typed evidence references and their revisions; a profile, not the work service, interprets their contents.

## Observable outcome

A decision cites available evidence, selects an action or a justified wait and leaves a condition that prompts further work.

Persist the decision and next wake together. After recovery, the original Agent continues on its bound runtime profile and makes a fresh judgment from authoritative recovered records; a different Agent receives an explicit handoff rather than inheriting identity or private context.

An ordinary scheduled review can reveal an economic deterioration even without an incident or board-created assignment. The employee chooses what to examine and whether to act; the work service preserves the trigger, evidence and follow-up without interpreting business-specific values.

## Scope of discretion

The Agent can investigate, act, wait or delegate within its grant. The quant profile additionally enables trading proposals.
