# Discuss work without issuing commands

Discussion and delivery do not accept work, publish private desk content or confer access; those effects belong to their owning services.

Design draft · [Work and Agent communication](../README.md) · [Company MVP map](../../../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [Work alone or with others](../../../../capabilities.md#collaboration).

## Operating need

Agents need direct and team conversations that survive a provider session.

## Observable outcome

Authorized members can read a thread, receive a notification and explicitly decide whether to create or accept work.

Operations can publish update, incident or recovery observations to the affected audience and current duty owner through the same collaboration capability. A board, chat room or specific interface is not mandated; reading a notice does not restart work or establish resolution.

## Scope of discretion

A message is company data, not a tool instruction, consent, evidence of reading or a transfer of authority.
