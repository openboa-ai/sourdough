# Request and track product maintenance

Design draft · [Company operations](../README.md) · [Company map](../../../../README.md)

## Operating need

A company can discover a defect in a product foundation or third-party dependency that it is not authorized to replace. It must seek a repair and continue tracking the original duty without human daily coordination.

## Observable outcome

An authorized, disclosure-checked request is linked to an external issue; a confirmed release, local application and resolution of the original problem remain separate recorded states. Relevant Agents receive the outcome through existing collaboration.

## Scope of discretion

The Agent can investigate and propose a fix, but cannot directly change the protected product foundation or disclose restricted company assets. Existing delegation governs external publication, destination, spend and release adoption. A human uses Client when a new decision outside that delegation is necessary.
