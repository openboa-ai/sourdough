# Verify and operate a company-owned change

Design draft · [Company operations](../README.md) · [Company map](../../../../README.md)

## Operating need

Agents need to improve company-owned programs and permitted extensions without requiring a human for ordinary work, while the company retains control of the effective version and its obligations.

## Observable outcome

A candidate has an accountable owner, independent evidence, a bounded activation, a known effective version and a recoverable disposition. Results reach the current work owner through company collaboration; a message is not activation.

## Scope of discretion

An Agent may propose, develop or delegate a change within its grant. The author cannot issue its own independent verdict or expand its own permissions. Changes to protected Sourdough product foundations are requested through [product maintenance](../request-maintenance/intent.md), not directly installed by an employee. Strategy economics and learning improvements have separate evidence owners.
