Business situation: [Steward resources and explain outcomes](../../../../capabilities.md#stewardship).

## Operating need

Operating costs and trading events need one auditable financial book, not totals reconstructed from chats or orders.

## Observable outcome

Source documents map to balanced postings and immutable corrections.

## Scope of discretion

The company owns its chart and posting rules. A fill subledger proves quantities and fees; it is not a second general ledger.
