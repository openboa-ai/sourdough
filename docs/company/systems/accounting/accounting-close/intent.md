# Reconcile and close a reporting period

Design draft · [Accounting and financial records](../README.md) · [Company MVP map](../../../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [Steward resources and explain outcomes](../../../../capabilities.md#stewardship).

## Operating need

Balanced entries alone do not show that cash and obligations match independent observations.

## Observable outcome

A closing record explains scoped discrepancies, closing balances and the journal cutoff.

Coverage includes expected operating-cost evidence even during a period with no business effects. Missing evidence is an open obligation, not a zero expense.

## Scope of discretion

Closing does not authorize a payment, order or edit to historical entries.
