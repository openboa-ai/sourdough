# Company

[Employee](../employee/README.md) · [Business situations](../capabilities.md) · [Implementing systems](systems/README.md)

The company is the continuing organization. It holds a mandate, employs workers, owns assets and records, and remains accountable for unresolved duties. The board sets direction and delegates limits; it does not run ordinary shifts.

- **Provide direction and capacity.** Decide what the company may do and afford, and whether a staffing need warrants hiring, reassignment, automation or waiting.
- **Maintain responsibility.** Record who accepted each duty, who can authorize an effect, who holds an asset and who must act next. Authority can end while an obligation remains.
- **Continue and account.** Preserve outcomes when workers or processes change, reconcile actuals and report what is known or still uncertain.
- **Specialize when necessary.** The [quant profile](profiles/quant/README.md) adds investment evidence, risk rules and return measures. The company does not need it to staff work, collaborate, manage operating funds or recover.

The [systems](systems/README.md) implement company responsibilities. The [Agent](../employee/agent/README.md) implements the employee side. Neither is a new organizational actor alongside Company and Employee.
