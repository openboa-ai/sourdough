# Operate a company-owned strategy program

Business situation: [Choose and perform work](../../../../../capabilities.md#judgment).

## Operating need

Agents can jointly develop a trading program and let it act under the firm's continuing supervision.

## Observable outcome

Each program order points to a company asset, pinned revision, deployment grant, responsible Agent and ordinary service admission.

A candidate can be compared with the current company operating revision under the research comparison contract; its promotion decision and its deployment are distinct.

Registration, independent evidence, authorized activation and each subsequent risk admission are distinct. A permitted Agent can also submit a direct order without first creating a strategy program. Strategy versions are company assets; roles and program decomposition are examples rather than mandatory organization.

## Scope of discretion

Writing code, evaluating it and granting a running deployment are distinct permissions.
