# Register and compare operating revisions

Design draft · Verification **UNRUN** · Responsible component: Research service registers the comparison; quant performance produces each arm's independent statement; an authorized company actor decides adoption. [Feature intent](../intent.md) · [Net-return trial](../../../../../../acceptance/net-return/spec.md)

## Boundary and requirements

**FR-1:** After a registered first operating revision has a prospective company-net measurement, record the incumbent and candidate before their new paired observation window. The earlier absolute result may be positive, negative or incomplete: it does not itself establish improvement. The candidate can be proposed earlier, but the paired comparison must use future observations not selected based on either arm's result.

**FR-2:** Register per arm the company/account binding, responsible Agent and immutable execution profile, strategy artifact and dependencies, effective research/memory/method revisions, granted tools and limits, operating triggers, permitted change policy, data and fill policy, capital, costs and valuation scope. Freeze the configuration or preregister bounded changes and effective-time receipts; unexpected changes invalidate affected attribution. Neither arm may obtain the other's future observations or sealed evaluation labels. Both may use Agent-written deterministic programs.

**FR-3:** Pin comparison window, primary metric, worthwhile margin, risk and cost ceilings, uncertainty method, shared-cost allocation, eligible source coverage, stopping rule and what changed. Give each arm separate isolated account state and financial records with common market observation times and comparable constraints. Account for attributable research and operation costs, including failed work, without double-counting shared actuals. Performance independently reconstructs each arm's statements under the existing return contract; research never maintains a second return calculator.

Each trial arm runs in an explicitly identified simulated evaluation namespace with distinct credentials, account, work queue, provider session, writable memory and artifacts. Copy only permitted, as-of baseline inputs. Neither arm can alter the actual operating company, the other's state, grants, active methods or sealed labels; live-capital destinations are unreachable. Include real trial usage in company cost accounting and apply the predeclared allocation to the two comparative statements without duplicating the actual expense. A shared immutable feed may be used when its time visibility is identical.

**FR-4:** Retain all attempts, decisions, context and revision lineage. A company actor with the applicable grant accepts, rejects or defers based on sealed results; without authority, coverage or a supported gain, keep the incumbent subject to its risk limits. Activation and rollback use the strategy deployment contract and cannot change prior orders or relax risk limits. A favorable return does not waive safety checks.

**NFR:** Do not claim attribution to one component when multiple components changed. Preserve failed, incomplete and adverse comparisons; a smaller loss may be an improvement but is not absolute profit. An absent candidate permits an incumbent absolute measurement, not a fabricated relative verdict.

## Independent evidence

| Case | Required observation |
| --- | --- |
| Baseline then comparison | Independently reconstruct the initial company-net statement. Register candidate and incumbent before their next common window; compare both statements, sources and pinned revisions with a separate worksheet. Observe authorized adopt or retain and the effective version on a later shift. |
| Drift or incomplete cost | Change a method revision mid-window outside the registered policy or omit an expected model cost. Invalidate affected attribution or report incomplete; do not select a favorable subwindow or activate the candidate. Restore coverage through a new linked revision and retain the prior result. |
| Authority and failure | A candidate exceeds the sealed risk limit or lacks a promotion grant. It remains inactive even with a positive return; the incumbent or a safe suspension remains effective. |
| Isolation and cost | Attempt a cross-arm write, production effect, label read and copied provider-session reuse; each is denied or invalidates the trial. Reconcile actual trial billing once and independently reproduce both statements using the registered cost allocation. |

No threshold in this draft authorizes real capital or implies statistical significance from a short sample.
