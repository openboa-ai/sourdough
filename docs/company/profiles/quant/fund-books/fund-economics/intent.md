# Explain net company performance

Business situation: [Steward resources and explain outcomes](../../../../../capabilities.md#stewardship).

## Operating need

The board and Agents need to know whether a method added value after trading and operating costs.

## Observable outcome

A versioned report separates trading and company net P&L, period and cumulative return, external flows and missing inputs. The performance projection returns these figures at the same revision for inspection. See [P1](../../../../../acceptance/episodes.md#p1-total-return).

Join investment-book facts, marks and company accounting actuals at a declared cut; missing sources or costs remain incomplete, not zero. Measure the first prospective paper operating revision before a matched candidate comparison. Paper performance, realized profit and recursive learning are separate claims.

## Scope of discretion

The firm may report a loss or an inconclusive evaluation. Positive returns alone cannot establish product readiness.

P1 qualifies the measuring instrument, not the business. The [net-return comparison](../../../../../acceptance/net-return/spec.md) uses these same statements and an independent reconstruction; it must not create a second definition of return.
