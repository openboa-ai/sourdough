# Run and recover a current-market paper account

Design draft · Verification **UNRUN** · Responsible components: market observation service for feed; independently durable simulated venue for virtual execution; shared trading core for admission, reconciliation and books; company accounting for actual operation costs. [Feature intent](../intent.md) · [Shared environment binding](../../execution-environment/bind/spec.md) · [Performance trial](../../../../../../acceptance/net-return/spec.md)

## Boundary and inputs

Before a forward window register the public production market-data source, product/instrument, market and availability timestamps, schema, continuity/age limits, source revision, and a separate paper execution binding and virtual starting account. Production market data is read-only; paper has no real-order credential or route. Pin core (`nautilus_trader==1.231.0`) and strategy artifact, risk mandate, observation cadence, virtual execution, fees, funding, margin and valuation policies, model assumptions, period, time source and cost coverage. **Spot execution** is the verified default on pinned `nautilus_trader==1.231.0` using a shared read-only public Binance mainnet market-data observation stream fanned out to isolated `SandboxExecutionClient` instances; for **perpetual** paper runs, Sourdough's accounting and reconciliation layer deterministically books 8-hour funding cash flows from live public funding-rate and mark-price observations until a newer runtime version is explicitly qualified. Pin identical conservative fill, latency, fee, tick/lot and one-way netting rules across historical replay and forward paper, and prohibit promoting a bar-execution backtest directly to live tick execution without quote/tick replay parity verification. No unqualified component may be represented as implemented or approved merely because it has been named.

The initial paper-window registry consumes explicit revision pins and numerical limits supplied by the existing operating mandate. It checks the active paper binding and its exposure ceiling, snapshots the bound capital and account identity, and commits the exact plan before its opening time. The registration timestamp is sampled after that commit returns. Only a durable plan with a recorded pre-opening timestamp is eligible; an interrupted or late commit with no qualifying seal admits no observations. A new request may not overlap a previously sealed window on that account. A request ID cannot change its terms; an exact retry of a sealed request returns the original receipt, even after the window. Only observations available within the recorded window are eligible. Registration does not start an Agent, qualify the feed, grant budget or trading authority, or establish cost coverage.

The scheduled valuation path derives nonoverlapping slots from the sealed opening and cadence. Each slot extends to the nanosecond before the next begins, capped at the inclusive closing instant. Its receipt ID is derived from the window revision and slot index. The original quote event, quote availability and observed account cut must be within that slot; the corroborating trade must be in the registered window. Only the first eligible capture for a slot is stored. An exact retry reads it, even after expiry; an expired missing slot is never backfilled. A read-only coverage report identifies the original revisions and missing slot ranges. Individual slot coverage is not proof of continuous feed coverage, sealed boundary NAV, or company-net return.

The final slot qualifies only when the quote's original event and availability can still support the declared closing instant under the managed freshness limit. The sampler waits until such evidence can exist instead of sealing an earlier, unusable final-slot receipt. Earlier receipts continue to reopen unchanged; a stale final receipt from a prior version still leaves closing valuation unavailable, not rewritten.

A trusted managed-paper setup command can seal a plan file against the original venue binding. A bounded managed company activation may then attach a sampler for that exact window. The sampler uses the same process-owned company writer lease for immutable receipts and reads the separate venue journal even while an employee turn is in progress. It never submits orders or changes grants, the Agent budget, or the normal shutdown deadline. Before the opening it waits; after the closing or worker shutdown it stops. On a supervised worker replacement the same window ID is retained. Sampling resumes at the current slot, without changing earlier receipts or filling a missed slot. Fixture and replay destinations cannot attach this sampler.

A plan may explicitly cap Agent turns across all managed activations attached to that window. Seal the current durable company run count with the cap before opening. Immediately before work starts under the writer lease, apply both the activation's existing turn budget and the remaining window ceiling. Reopening the same registration or launching a new supervisor does not reset either the sealed count or consumed attempts. A failed turn still consumes its durable run; count other admitted company runs conservatively against the same ceiling. A store behind the sealed count cannot run that window. Exhaustion starts no new Agent turn and does not discard reconciliation or valuation. Plans without a ceiling retain the existing observation-only behavior. The cap is not a grant, cash charge, monetary budget, or replacement for supervising to the window deadline.

For a capped window, a managed supervisor can set an explicit `--observe-through-ns` cutoff for one bounded observation segment. The cutoff must be inside the sealed window, no more than 600 seconds ahead, and leave time for the existing supervisor to finish. Preserve that absolute cutoff on worker replacement. After successful work or turn exhaustion, continue collecting eligible marks under the same writer lease until the cutoff; do not reopen a completed turn. An exceptional work exit still stops the sampler promptly. Without this option, sampler lifetime remains tied to ordinary worker work. This cutoff bounds observation, not order authority or the lifetime of an already running turn. A higher-level operating runner still owns successive segment launches.

`run-paper-window` sequences those segments for a sealed, capped window. Require the currently bound paper account and its exact exposure ceiling to match the plan. Pin the company store, window revision and independent venue journal identity before starting; recheck before each segment. Use the existing managed venue supervisor, wait for its original readiness, then start the existing company supervisor on the same window. Do not start another segment until both processes finish and the venue has drained its claimed effect. A missing readiness or unsuccessful exit stops the sequence, with the observed cutoff and incomplete slot coverage retained; the runner does not substitute a venue, backfill a mark, renew Agent allowance, or schedule a new segment after the window. Reserve 150 seconds of runway before starting a turn to accommodate the bounded employee exchange and paper submission. With less time remaining, observe and reconcile without starting a fresh turn. This reservation is not a financial admission policy or a guarantee of dispatch completion. Completion here describes supervision, with coverage reported separately; it is not economic success or authorization of a live destination.

The [runtime integration qualification contract](../../execution-environment/qualify-engine/spec.md) requires durable company admission before dispatch and a **two-process paper venue boundary** that survives company loss: the managed paper execution worker runs in a separate process from `CompanyRuntime` under `paper_sender_lease` with bounded rotating activation segments (8–600 seconds) and explicit drain margins. A process-local order cache or assumed no-fill during downtime is not an independently witnessed virtual venue.

## Requirements

- **FR-1:** Stream observations after registration and persist raw source identity, sequence, event/availability timestamps, corrections, and coverage. Validate instrument/units; detect duplicate, gap, stale stream and clock uncertainty. Reconnect under bounded retry and backfill only where the source can prove continuity, preserving when each event actually became available. A missing gap stays visible; do not backdate a recovery or use a later event in an earlier Agent decision.
- **FR-2:** Invoke the same company strategy, authorization, risk, order, posting, accounting and projection contracts as external execution. Send admitted orders only through the paper binding to an independent, durable simulated venue. It supplies stable original order/fill identities, partial/unfilled/rejected outcomes, fees, funding and cash/position observations according to the pinned model. A simulated fill uses only information available by its simulated fill time and cannot claim queue position or liquidity it did not observe.
- **FR-3:** When data age or continuity exceeds the registered policy, block affected risk increases and unsafe simulated fills; retain outstanding orders, cancel/lookup and risk-reducing operations where authorized and modelable. Resume only from verified data coverage and reconcile the original paper account. Never switch to a test or production execution endpoint to fill a gap.
- **FR-4:** On company process loss, the venue journal survives independently. Reopen the same store and paper binding; reconcile original order and fill IDs, post once, resume the Agent's next duty and preserve pending accounting events. Data-source reconnect cannot create another order. Venue-loss recovery must be separately qualified and cannot be inferred from company-process restart.
- **FR-5:** Feed market marks and paper account cash movements into the existing investment books; reconcile actual Agent, research, data and infrastructure cost receipts through company accounting. Publish separate modeled execution costs and actual company expense coverage. Missing fees, funding, marks or billed costs makes the affected net result incomplete, never zero.
- **FR-6:** Register a model-validation plan for the exact product and supported order types: compare recorded simulated execution prices and timing with independently captured, contemporaneous production quotes and trades where available; compare fee, funding and margin inputs with their declared sources. Report coverage, spread/slippage assumptions and adverse-cost sensitivity. Public quotes and trades do not prove the simulated order's actual queue position, fill probability or market impact; do not label the simulated fill as a verified real fill. An external test venue's fill alone does not calibrate production execution.
- **NFR:** A fill is an estimate, not an assertion that the real venue would execute it. State the data granularity and unsupported order/margin/liquidation behavior; deny unsupported effects rather than inventing a model. Record actual run IDs, disconnects, omissions and failed attempts. Do not treat a fixed-price fault fixture or testnet price alone as the registered current-market paper result.

## Acceptance and independent evidence

| Case | Required observation |
| --- | --- |
| Forward control | Against an independently observed, read-only current source, capture feed and receive times, generate an actual Agent or pinned program decision, exercise paper admission and record order/fill/account/expense lineage. Verify source freshness under a threshold sealed before activation and independently reconstruct cash, position and cost. No production order calls. |
| Feed gap | Drop and redeliver observations, then reconnect with a late correction. Preserve the original as-of decision, mark the uncovered interval and deny a dependent risk increase; retain cancellation and reconciliation. If continuity cannot be proven, do not report a continuous qualified period. |
| Post-fill crash | Commit a virtual fill at the independently durable venue, kill the company before receipt, restart automatically, query the original ID and book it once. Compare the company journal and the venue's independent book; the same Agent reads the resulting position on its next duty. |
| Cost or model limitation | Omit a required funding/cost receipt or request an unsupported order type. Report an incomplete company-net result or explicit denial, not a zero cost or optimistic fill. |
| Turn ceiling across activations | Commit one attempted run under the sealed ceiling, interrupt it before a decision, then launch a new bounded activation on the same window. Verify that the second activation reuses the original run-count baseline, can reconcile existing work, and starts no new turn once the ceiling is exhausted. |
| Observation after work ends | Exhaust the window's Agent allowance before a late slot. Under a valid supervised cutoff, observe the late slot without another Agent invocation. Reject cutoffs outside the sealed window or beyond the bounded activation. |
| Automatic segment transition | Start one capped managed paper window, observe one ready venue and company activation close, then a different venue generation on the original account. Observe the second cutoff without any Client or driver action between segments. A failed venue qualification does not start a company process or pick another account. |
| Model sensitivity | Replay identical recorded market observations with the sealed base and adverse spread, delay and fill assumptions. Report both net outcomes and source coverage independently; do not pick the favorable case as the only result or present observed public prints as proof the paper order really filled. |

The first local fault test can use a fixed synthetic feed for repeatability; this spec's forward-market evidence requires an actual separately observed current feed and is not satisfied by that fixture.

## Recurring decisions

On a recurring managed paper execution duty, resolve the current account's instrument and order sizing before asking the Agent for its decision. The authorized result exposes the instrument, whole lots and base units per lot, quote scale, price step, minimum lots and minimum notional. The current quote is in integer quote units per lot and the reported position counts lots. This projection does not rewrite the preceding book observation or relax admission checks. A destination without an explicit lot contract retains its own existing unit names.

## Recovering the bounded spot cash worker

The company supervises the sandbox independently of the Agent worker. On sandbox restart, discard the previous generation's quote. Reconcile all original completed orders against the durable fill and virtual balance receipts and start the same account from that verified cash and spot holding, not its configured initial capital. The replacement must independently observe those balances in the SDK and obtain a fresh current quote before readiness. A queued order never claimed by a sender retains its original identity. An order possibly sent without conclusive evidence, an unsupported partial result, or a missing balance receipt prevents activation and does not authorize resubmission. This is a cash-spot checkpoint, not recovery of a general-purpose matching engine or evidence of testnet behavior.

If the quote expires between company admission and sandbox enqueue, persist the exact original command as queued. A queue row is not a market send, a rejection, or proof of a fill. Keep the company reservation, and require a fresh quote from the claiming generation before any send. Recovery drains the one durable command rather than retrying it under a different action or synthesizing terminal evidence.

The cash-spot worker uses the original SDK quote event and availability timestamps plus a corroborating source trade. Neither callback execution nor an unrelated newer trade resets the quote age. Both quote timestamps must be ordered and within the existing 15-second limit. An older SDK journal without the event timestamp cannot authorize current-market admission from that quote; the replacement waits for a fresh observation.

The managed worker claims only one command at a time until the original terminal history and SDK account checkpoint agree. At activation expiry it withdraws the quote and stops claiming queued commands, then gives the already claimed order up to five seconds to reach that checkpoint. A late native callback and corroborated balance may complete the original order during this drain. If they do not, the worker reports an unresolved shutdown rather than a clean exit; it does not invent an account receipt, release exposure, or send another order. Unclaimed commands keep their original IDs.
