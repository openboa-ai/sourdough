# Run and recover a current-market paper account

Design draft · Verification **UNRUN** · Responsible components: market observation service for feed; independently durable simulated venue for virtual execution; shared trading core for admission, reconciliation and books; company accounting for actual operation costs. [Feature intent](../intent.md) · [Shared environment binding](../../execution-environment/bind/spec.md) · [Performance trial](../../../../../../acceptance/net-return/spec.md)

## Boundary and inputs

Before a forward window register the public production market-data source, product/instrument, market and availability timestamps, schema, continuity/age limits, source revision, and a separate paper execution binding and virtual starting account. Production market data is read-only; paper has no real-order credential or route. Pin core and strategy artifact, risk mandate, observation cadence, virtual execution, fees, funding, margin and valuation policies, model assumptions, period, time source and cost coverage. No unqualified component may be represented as implemented or approved merely because it has been named.

The [runtime integration qualification contract](../../execution-environment/qualify-engine/spec.md) requires durable company admission before dispatch and a virtual venue that survives company loss. A process-local order cache or assumed no-fill during downtime is not an independently witnessed virtual venue.

## Requirements

- **FR-1:** Stream observations after registration and persist raw source identity, sequence, event/availability timestamps, corrections, and coverage. Validate instrument/units; detect duplicate, gap, stale stream and clock uncertainty. Reconnect under bounded retry and backfill only where the source can prove continuity, preserving when each event actually became available. A missing gap stays visible; do not backdate a recovery or use a later event in an earlier Agent decision.
- **FR-2:** Invoke the same company strategy, authorization, risk, order, posting, accounting and projection contracts as external execution. Send admitted orders only through the paper binding to an independent, durable simulated venue. It supplies stable original order/fill identities, partial/unfilled/rejected outcomes, fees, funding and cash/position observations according to the pinned model. A simulated fill uses only information available by its simulated fill time and cannot claim queue position or liquidity it did not observe.
- **FR-3:** When data age or continuity exceeds the registered policy, block affected risk increases and unsafe simulated fills; retain outstanding orders, cancel/lookup and risk-reducing operations where authorized and modelable. Resume only from verified data coverage and reconcile the original paper account. Never switch to a test or production execution endpoint to fill a gap.
- **FR-4:** On company process loss, the venue journal survives independently. Reopen the same store and paper binding; reconcile original order and fill IDs, post once, resume the Agent's next duty and preserve pending accounting events. Data-source reconnect cannot create another order. Venue-loss recovery must be separately qualified and cannot be inferred from company-process restart.
- **FR-5:** Feed market marks and paper account cash movements into the existing investment books; reconcile actual Agent, research, data and infrastructure cost receipts through company accounting. Publish separate modeled execution costs and actual company expense coverage. Missing fees, funding, marks or billed costs makes the affected net result incomplete, never zero.
- **FR-6:** Register a model-validation plan for the exact product and supported order types: compare recorded simulated execution prices and timing with independently captured, contemporaneous production quotes and trades where available; compare fee, funding and margin inputs with their declared sources. Report coverage, spread/slippage assumptions and adverse-cost sensitivity. Public quotes and trades do not prove the simulated order's actual queue position, fill probability or market impact; do not label the simulated fill as a verified real fill. An external test venue's fill alone does not calibrate production execution.
- **NFR:** A fill is an estimate, not an assertion that the real venue would execute it. State the data granularity and unsupported order/margin/liquidation behavior; deny unsupported effects rather than inventing a model. Record actual run IDs, disconnects, omissions and failed attempts. Do not treat a fixed-price fault fixture or testnet price alone as the registered current-market paper result.

## Acceptance and independent evidence

| Case | Required observation |
| --- | --- |
| Forward control | Against an independently observed, read-only current source, capture feed and receive times, generate an actual Agent or pinned program decision, exercise paper admission and record order/fill/account/expense lineage. Verify source freshness under a threshold sealed before activation and independently reconstruct cash, position and cost. No production order calls. |
| Feed gap | Drop and redeliver observations, then reconnect with a late correction. Preserve the original as-of decision, mark the uncovered interval and deny a dependent risk increase; retain cancellation and reconciliation. If continuity cannot be proven, do not report a continuous qualified period. |
| Post-fill crash | Commit a virtual fill at the independently durable venue, kill the company before receipt, restart automatically, query the original ID and book it once. Compare the company journal and the venue's independent book; the same Agent reads the resulting position on its next duty. |
| Cost or model limitation | Omit a required funding/cost receipt or request an unsupported order type. Report an incomplete company-net result or explicit denial, not a zero cost or optimistic fill. |
| Model sensitivity | Replay identical recorded market observations with the sealed base and adverse spread, delay and fill assumptions. Report both net outcomes and source coverage independently; do not pick the favorable case as the only result or present observed public prints as proof the paper order really filled. |

The first local fault test can use a fixed synthetic feed for repeatability; this spec's forward-market evidence requires an actual separately observed current feed and is not satisfied by that fixture.

## Recurring decisions

On a recurring managed paper execution duty, resolve the current account's instrument and order sizing before asking the Agent for its decision. The authorized result exposes the instrument, whole lots and base units per lot, quote scale, price step, minimum lots and minimum notional. The current quote is in integer quote units per lot and the reported position counts lots. This projection does not rewrite the preceding book observation or relax admission checks. A destination without an explicit lot contract retains its own existing unit names.

## Recovering the bounded spot cash worker

The company supervises the sandbox independently of the Agent worker. On sandbox restart, discard the previous generation's quote. Reconcile all original completed orders against the durable fill and virtual balance receipts and start the same account from that verified cash and spot holding, not its configured initial capital. The replacement must independently observe those balances in the SDK and obtain a fresh current quote before readiness. A queued order never claimed by a sender retains its original identity. An order possibly sent without conclusive evidence, an unsupported partial result, or a missing balance receipt prevents activation and does not authorize resubmission. This is a cash-spot checkpoint, not recovery of a general-purpose matching engine or evidence of testnet behavior.

If the quote expires between company admission and sandbox enqueue, persist the exact original command as queued. A queue row is not a market send, a rejection, or proof of a fill. Keep the company reservation, and require a fresh quote from the claiming generation before any send. Recovery drains the one durable command rather than retrying it under a different action or synthesizing terminal evidence.
