# Qualify the selected trading-runtime integration

Design draft · **UNRUN** · Owner: trading integration. [Environment intent](../intent.md) · [Core binding](../bind/spec.md) · [Parity acceptance](../../../../../../acceptance/execution-parity/spec.md) · [Current-market paper](../../live-paper/run/spec.md) · [Historical replay](../../../investments/backtest/replay/spec.md)

## Objective and boundary

The planned trading runtime is NautilusTrader. Sourdough provisions, starts and configures it through the [company-managed runtime lifecycle](../launch/spec.md), reusing its data, strategy, backtesting, simulated and exchange-execution functions. The company does not implement a second trading engine. The company's integration owns authority, original action identity, idempotency, retry and recovery across the runtime and venue boundary; these responsibilities are not a requirement that the runtime implement Sourdough's full company contract itself. No runtime is installed, qualified or activated by this document.

Market observations and execution destinations are independently bound: current production data is read-only for paper, historical data is causal for replay, and a separately authorized non-production account tests the actual venue adapter. A simulated venue used in the post-fill acceptance must survive the killed company service and expose an independently observable durable order/fill history. A same-process simulated order cache or an assumed unfilled order after restart is insufficient. A real external account cannot be used as the fault fixture or reached with paper authority.

## FR: integration contract

- **FR-1 Data:** Pin source and product, symbol mapping, event and availability times, precision, gap detection, reconnect and actual freshness. Record whether historical funding, trades or order-book evidence exists. A guessed bar path or missing arrival timestamp is an assumption, not an observed fill.
- **FR-2 Shared behavior:** Pin the selected runtime and company integration revisions and use the same company authorization, risk, action identity, reservation, order, accounting and projection contracts for replay, paper and the external adapter. Capture the strategy and runtime entry-point fingerprints, clock and actual call traces. Similar strategy class names alone do not prove one execution path. Where runtime modes have separate implementations, demonstrate the same Sourdough-owned invariant boundary; a different result or unsupported behavior remains a gap.
- **FR-3 Original effect:** The company integration synchronously commits action, account/environment binding, immutable terms, reservation and dispatch obligation before allowing a write to reach a venue through the runtime. An asynchronous runtime enqueue, log, callback or in-memory order ID is not a company durable commit. The company fences stale senders, reuses the original identity and preserves unknown outcomes across process death. Do not blindly resend or infer absent/flat from empty lookup.
- **FR-4 Independent paper venue:** For the declared post-fill acceptance, witness a virtual fill commit at a destination that survives the killed company service, restart through the product supervisor and query the original venue identity. Runtime-local cache restore or a journal in the killed process is not that witness. Qualify whether the selected runtime can host its simulated venue in a separately surviving process with queryable durable state. If not, record the gap and evaluate the smallest integration needed; do not create or promise a replacement trading engine.
- **FR-5 Account and risk:** Show server-side isolation of paper, non-production external and live order credentials, accounts and positions. Validate the selected product's contract units, account/position and margin modes, limits, fees, funding and risk events; fail closed on unsupported or unknown semantics. A live data connection must not grant live execution. Do not rely solely on the runtime's default account routing, risk bypass or unavailable-position handling.
- **FR-6 Decision and economics:** Independently reconstruct the fill, fees, signed funding, account cash, position, company operating costs and return. Declare execution, latency, spread, liquidity and margin assumptions, sensitivity and provenance. A favorable backtest or forward paper result is not proof of actual market fills or live profit.
- **FR-7 Qualification:** Record release/commit and license, actual feature support, integration surface, platform compatibility, access and operational cost, unverified areas, integration/maintenance effort and rollback path. These are due-diligence inputs, not permission to install, use credentials or release. Do not copy third-party code without separate license review and required notices.
- **FR-8 Authority without duplication:** Identify whether the runtime's qualified durable store can be the company's one authoritative record or whether a small company-owned admission/ledger boundary is required. If both retain state, identify authoritative records and reconciliation by stable source ID; a cached estimate must not compete with the company position or independently dispatch around its grant and reservation. Reuse the runtime's matching, data, strategy and venue functions whenever their observed contracts suffice.

## Qualification observations

| Case | Required evidence and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Mode and lineage | One pinned strategy and common core traverse causal historical replay and current-feed paper, producing matching risk/ledger contracts on equivalent fixed inputs. Capture actual artifact digests and events, not only an interface declaration. Unsupported mode or separate behavior without proven conformance is **INCOMPLETE**. |
| Pre-send persistence cut | Cut after durable company admission and before send, then separately after possible venue acceptance but before local acknowledgment/commit. An independent observer compares committed company action, destination request count and recovery query. No second logical order; an unprovable external result remains unknown. |
| Paper post-fill cut | Independent virtual venue commits the fill; block company delivery; kill only the company process. Product supervisor reopens the original company and one original order, observes the same fill, books it once and triggers the same Agent's new duty. Run founder and hired-Agent integrations separately. Same-process replay or manually restarting the product does not pass. |
| Late history and stale inputs | Exercise duplicate and delayed fills, incomplete terminal fill history, provider unavailability, feed gaps, fee or funding correction and stale generation. Do not release risk or calculate a complete net return without required evidence. |
| External account effect | Inject a separately observed venue-originated forced effect and an unattributed order; verify original provenance, one posting, exposure containment and no invented Agent command. Missing position history is unknown. Apply [external-effect reconciliation](../../recover-order/reconcile-external/spec.md). |
| Environment containment | With real market observations enabled, attempt a paper order against a forbidden real execution endpoint or credential. Verify independently that no such write occurs; test account switching with an unresolved original order. |
| External non-production | Under separate authorization, test the exact selected account/product with independent venue observations. Distinguish unknown from rejected, partial from filled and durable order identity from a transport response. The test venue's market prices or simulator semantics are not assumed to match the production market. |
| Validation | Record a case-by-case result for the selected runtime and company integration: **SOURCE_SUPPORTED**, **OBSERVED_PASS**, **FAIL**, **INCOMPLETE**, **BLOCKED** or **UNRUN**, with build, environment, input, independent oracle, evidence and last result. A source claim or fixture cannot be reclassified as actual integration evidence. |

## Decision rule

Use the selected runtime rather than building a replacement. Qualify a pinned version and implement only the company lifecycle and safety integration needed around it. Record capability gaps rather than claiming success or silently switching engines. Runtime qualification is not the founder or hired-Agent integration pass and never authorizes live trading.
