# Employee

[Company](../company/README.md) · [Business situations](../capabilities.md) · [Software realization: Agent](agent/README.md)

An employee is the organizational participant who accepts duties, exercises judgment within delegated limits and answers for results. A role describes responsibility, not permission. The company owns the assets, records and outstanding obligations.

| Situation | Employee responsibility | Company responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Join or take on a role | Accept a first duty within scope. | Justify the staffing choice and record actual grants and allocation; a roster entry does not prove readiness. |
| Work and collaborate | Choose an approach, keep a scoped working set, publish selectively and accept or decline handoffs. | Preserve authoritative work, custody and audience records. Delivery does not transfer a duty. |
| Learn | Propose and evaluate a method; personal use does not set company policy. | Preserve trial evidence and enforce the scope of adoption. |
| Recover, hand off or leave | Resume verified work or hand it to a separately authorized successor. Historical authorship stays put. | Fence obsolete writers, settle custody and retain an owner for unfinished work. |

In Sourdough an **Agent** realizes the employee. The employment record refers to its existing Agent ID; this view introduces no extra employee ID, table or service. Configuration, Harness, Desk and Learning belong under Agent. Restarting a session on the same binding may preserve the Agent; another runtime means another Agent, even when the role stays the same.
