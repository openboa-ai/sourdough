# Does an unattended quant fund earn its cost?

Design draft · **UNRUN** · Integration owner: Quant performance; research registers trials, risk enforces the mandate, accounting supplies actuals, an independent observer reconstructs the result. Paper only. [Business intent](../../intent.md) · [Experiment](../../company/profiles/quant/investments/research/experiment/spec.md) · [Return convention](../../company/profiles/quant/fund-books/fund-economics/total-return/spec.md)

## Two questions, one set of books

1. **Absolute goal:** was company net P&L positive, and was company-net return above the predeclared hurdle within the risk budget? Use whole-company net NAV in a common reporting currency. Report signed profit and flow-adjusted time-weighted return (TWR); a contribution is not profit.
2. **Incremental system improvement:** did the candidate company operating revision improve on the incumbent after its costs, within the same mandate? Both belong to the Agent-run fund. Both may trade entirely through deterministic code. Retaining a profitable incumbent is a valid business outcome; a candidate that loses less has not achieved absolute profit.

## Registration and sequence

| Stage | Required action | Evidence ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instrument and operating checks | Pass the [P1 return oracle](../episodes.md#p1-total-return); qualify the unattended founder and hired-Agent paper paths, risk stops and complete cost capture. Freeze the trial before its forward clock starts. | Correct accounting and operation, not an investment result. |
| Historical research | Run [historical replay](../../company/profiles/quant/investments/backtest/replay/spec.md) chronologically with as-of data; separate tuning from evaluation, account for overlapping labels/holding periods at split boundaries, register all variants and failures. Stress fees, financing, spread, slippage and attainable size. | Can disqualify a strategy. Backtests, including favorable ones, never count as forward observations. |
| First operating revision | Register the founding Agent's company-owned operating revision and run [current-market paper](../../company/profiles/quant/trading/live-paper/run/spec.md) prospectively on read-only production-market observations arriving after registration, with virtual order execution. Close a company-net statement with independent venue and cost-source coverage. Report actual company costs and simulated investment profit, loss or incomplete; do not wait for a candidate or force a positive result. | An initial paper result, not a claim of improvement or realized live ROI. Fixed-price recovery fixtures and unqualified test-environment prices do not count. |
| Later paired comparison | After the first operating revision has a prospective company-net measurement, register incumbent and candidate for a new common current-market forward window. Give each its own paper account, book and attributable costs; use the same eligible instrument, start capital, external-flow schedule, observation times, execution assumptions and mandate ceilings. No board decisions. | A bounded comparison under simulated fills, not realized live ROI. A contrived price path or an unqualified test-environment fill is not prospective evidence about market returns. |
| Real capital | Not part of this design. Would require separate authorization, controls and independently reconciled actual statements and expenses over a new observation period. | No automatic promotion from paper or inference of enduring profitability. |

**Seal before observation:** identify the capital perimeter, currency, cadence and holding horizon, eligible data and availability times, all arms and policy digests, fixed start/end and minimum effective observations, turnover/liquidity assumptions, maximum drawdown and exposure, spending cap, minimum absolute and excess return, paired uncertainty method, multiple-trial adjustment and abort rule. Numerical business/risk limits must come from the authorized evaluation mandate, not be chosen after seeing returns. Unset inputs block a verdict. Pre-register how to handle corrections, outages and model changes; a changed claim starts a new trial.

Record simulation fidelity separately: data granularity and freshness, independently captured quotes/trades where available, source and versions for fees, funding, margin and execution model, and predeclared adverse spread, delay and liquidity sensitivity. Public market events do not prove that a hypothetical order would have filled. Unsupported behavior or missing source coverage prevents a stronger realism claim; a favorable paper P&L cannot by itself validate realized trading performance.

## Comparators and attribution

- **Cash/no trade** shows the cash opportunity and the cost of running an idle fund. Record actual/specified cash yield and its own unavoidable expenses; do not burden it with imaginary Agent spend.
- **Incumbent** is the company's current operating revision, frozen before comparison. If none exists, the founding Agent researches, writes and registers a first company-owned strategy; record its absolute results but do not claim a revision gain until a challenger exists. Freezing the policy and program does not freeze the decisions they can make. Pin the responsible Agent, strategy digest, decision authority, data and change policy.
- **Candidate** is the same company's proposed revision. Record the specific change and what remains fixed. For example, an Agent revises its rebalance rule to avoid orders whose expected benefit is below estimated cost; both revisions then trade in code without a per-order model call. Give both the same eligible universe, risk budget, fill model and observation window. Charge each its attributable research, operation and execution costs; allocate shared costs consistently, never twice in the company books. If several mechanisms change together, attribute the result only to the bundle.

Research owns the complete [operating-revision comparison record](../../company/profiles/quant/investments/research/compare-operating-revisions/spec.md); quant performance owns the statements and an authorized company actor owns the decision to adopt. The first absolute measurement and later paired comparison are separate windows and verdicts. RSI has its own [recursive trial](../recursive-learning/spec.md) and does not gate starting the first measurement.

## Independent oracle and verdict

- **FR:** Reconstruct trading NAV from fills, marks, fees and signed funding; reconcile external flows and company expenses (model, data, compute and infrastructure, including research and failed attempts) from company accounting. State allocation of shared or one-off costs in advance. No free Agent labor, double-counted execution shortfall, or subtraction of an expense both at accrual and payment. Reconcile independent reconstruction to each account and cost-inclusive statement revision; report missing items explicitly.
- **NFR:** Report company net P&L and linked TWR, excess over each comparator, peak-to-trough drawdown on the flow-adjusted series, exposure, turnover, cost breakdown, available periods and uncertainty. Compare paired returns on the same dates using the registered dependence-aware method (e.g. contiguous-block resampling); account for family selection. Do not treat each fill as independent evidence or stop early at a favorable result.
- **Verdict per question (paper only):** grade each operating revision's absolute result against positive company-net profit, the registered absolute and cash hurdles, and the risk limits. Separately, support the candidate change only if the lower uncertainty bound for its paired net-return difference over the incumbent exceeds the registered worthwhile margin within the risk limits. A bound spanning the margin is inconclusive; an upper bound below the margin does not support the change. A hard risk breach fails even with high returns. Incomplete costs or inadequate independent observations cannot establish improvement. Never backfill unknowns with zero.
- **Sanity case, not market evidence:** on 1,000 opening capital and no external flows, gross gain 12 less 4 trading costs and 10 operating costs gives the candidate a company loss of 2 and TWR −0.2%. Against the incumbent earning +0.1% net, the candidate trails by 0.3 percentage points; a green trading book is not company profit. Remove the model-cost receipt: trading return remains measurable, company-net verdict becomes unavailable.

At review retain the cash reference, the registered revisions' statements and all failed or aborted windows. If the change is unsupported or inconclusive, keep the incumbent within its mandate or suspend risk if required. Rejecting a change is not a verdict against Agent-run operation.

## Connected CI case

On a controlled clock, seal a managed paper window before observation, capture scheduled marks on both sides of an original venue fill, and close through the CLI. Reconstruct the marked trading loss from the independent venue records. Without the expected operating receipt, only trading is measurable; supplying that receipt permits a distinct corrected company-net result. Reopen both revisions after detaching the test venue and verify exact close redelivery. Controlled venue inputs and illustrative costs qualify this path, not public-feed operation, a business verdict, or P1's Client observations.
