# Founder and newly hired Agent: design trace

Design review · **UNRUN**. This is a static trace of proposed contracts, not a replay, product run, financial result or evidence of a verified runtime. [Founder acceptance](first-shift/spec.md) and [new hire acceptance](hired-agent-continuity/spec.md) remain separate and authoritative.

## Common path

| Step | Founder path | New hire path | Required witness and potential blocker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Authority and wake | A founded company has its pinned founder, grants and a due review. Supervisor and scheduler start an actual turn. | A real manager judges need and hires A with a pinned profile, bounded grants and allocation; A accepts its first assigned duty. | Company roster, grant, current claim and actual runtime tool receipts. An unavailable runtime, human consent prompt, budget or missing grant is **BLOCKED**, not a simulated pass. A legitimate decision not to hire leaves hire coverage **INCOMPLETE**. |
| 2 · Research and judgment | The founder chooses a question, reads decision-time observations and authors its own finding. | A independently chooses a question and authors its own finding within W1; the manager's report cannot substitute. | Snapshot availability, actual author/run generation, independently recalculated finding and subsequent rationale. [First finding](../company/profiles/quant/investments/research/decision-note/spec.md) may precede the trade decision; no seeded trade or future data. A justified hold is valid judgment but leaves the fill branch incomplete. |
| 3 · Admission and dispatch | Founder chooses an order based on that finding. | A chooses an order based on A's own finding. | Persist decision and original action ID, check grant and risk, atomically create one subledger reservation, then send through the bound paper connector. The [shared environment](../company/profiles/quant/trading/execution-environment/bind/spec.md) must identify destination and market source separately. Stale data, foreign binding or insufficient headroom denies dispatch. |
| 4 · Commit and cut | Independent paper venue commits one fill; withhold its reply and notification and kill the company before posting. | Same cut on A's actual order after the hire, before W1 settles. | Ordered venue commit, absent company receipt and observed process exit. Fixture or a thrown exception without an external durable venue commit is insufficient. Use one company/store/account per attempt. |
| 5 · Recover original | Product supervisor restarts the original company with the same founder profile. | Product supervisor restarts the original company and A retains its original profile. | Fence old generation; query original binding and ID; apply each fill and fee once; retain accounting handoff and reservation until evidence is complete. Unknown status stays reserved with a due lookup. A different Agent or manually triggered restart cannot pass. |
| 6 · New duty | A distinct later founder duty reads the reconciled position and makes a new decision. | A distinct W2 on the same A/profile reads the reconciled position and quantity and makes a new decision. | Authenticated read, fresh evidence, decision rationale and next responsibility. A resumed W1 or cached pre-crash explanation is not W2. |

Both paths require the full chain in a single continuous attempt, zero human/driver operating commands and independently witnessed venue and book facts. Keep each verdict separate; a company-only hired binding does not establish its paper binding.
