# Inspect authorized company state

Design draft · [Board Client](../README.md) · [System map](../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [Set direction and inspect it](../../capabilities.md#mandate).

## Operating need

A board member needs an executive-first explanation of portfolio performance, live trade execution, agent office activity, and company health while the company operates independently.

## Observable outcome

The client opens to live paper-trading performance and execution lineage, offers a living spatial Studio Floor Map with full-screen drill-down into run logs, read-only inter-agent discussions, and published reports, collapses internal technical metadata into Tier 2 disclosures or quiet system views, and displays permitted records with correct units, source revision, as-of time, and explicit unknowns. Inspection issues no operational command.

## Scope of discretion

Select, format, and navigate across Tier 0 executive views, Tier 1 causal drill-downs (zero right slide-over drawers), and quiet lateral Tier 2 system screens. Do not create records of fact, promote a method, wake a worker, peek into `.desk/drafts/*` bodies, or calculate a replacement financial result.
