# Inspect authorized company state

Design draft · [Board Client](../README.md) · [System map](../../README.md) · [Development lifecycle](../../engineering/lifecycle.md)

Business situation: [Set direction and inspect it](../../capabilities.md#mandate).

## Operating need

A board member needs an explanation of work while the company operates independently.

## Observable outcome

The client displays permitted records, correct units, source revision, as-of time and unknowns; inspection issues no operational command.

## Scope of discretion

Select, format and navigate. Do not create records of fact, promote a method, wake a worker or calculate a replacement financial result.
